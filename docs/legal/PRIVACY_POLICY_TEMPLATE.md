## 1. What we collect
- **Account data:** name, email, password (stored only as a salted hash).
- **Health and fitness data you enter:** age, sex, height, weight, goals, activity level, meals and foods logged, water, workouts, weight and body measurements, progress photos (if you add them).
- **Technical data:** device/browser type, IP address and session records, used for security and fraud prevention.
- **AI coach messages** you send, together with the fitness data needed to answer them.

Health-related information may be **special-category data** in some regions. We process it only to provide the app to you, based on your consent [confirm lawful basis with counsel].

## 2. How we use it
To provide and personalise the app, calculate your targets, show your progress, secure your account, improve the service, and (if enabled) generate AI coaching responses. [We do not sell personal data. We do not use health data for advertising.]

## 3. Who we share it with
Service providers that host and run ORVIX (hosting, database, [AI provider], [barcode/food data provider], [email provider]) under contract. We share the minimum needed. We may disclose data where legally required.

## 4. Retention
We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account in the app, we schedule permanent deletion of your data [within X days] and remove it from backups [within X days].

## 5. Your rights
Depending on where you live, you can access, correct, export, delete or restrict use of your data, and withdraw consent. In the app: **Profile → Security & data → Download my data / Delete my account**. Or contact [privacy email].

## 6. Security
Encryption in transit, hashed passwords, access controls and audit logging. No system is perfectly secure.

## 7. Children
ORVIX is not intended for people under 13 [or the age in your region]. We do not knowingly collect their data.

## 8. Not medical advice
ORVIX provides general fitness and nutrition information and estimates. It is not medical advice. Consult a doctor before changing your diet or exercise routine.

We will notify you of material changes. Questions: [privacy email].
